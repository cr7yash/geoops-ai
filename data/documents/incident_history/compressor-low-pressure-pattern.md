# Compressor Low-Pressure Incident Pattern

## Pattern summary

Across fourteen low-pressure incidents, the most common confirmed causes were leaking service fittings, restricted evaporator airflow, iced coils, and inaccurate pressure transducers. Compressor mechanical failure accounted for one incident and followed abnormal noise plus elevated winding temperature.

## Evidence to capture

Technicians should preserve controller alarm history, pressure trends, ambient temperature, line temperatures, manifold readings, airflow observations, leak-test results, and photographs of oil staining. This evidence distinguishes a true refrigerant loss from sensor drift or airflow restriction.

## Repeat-event handling

For repeat incidents on the same asset within ninety days, review the prior service report before adding refrigerant or replacing a transducer. Escalate recurring unexplained charge loss to reliability engineering for a full leak survey.
