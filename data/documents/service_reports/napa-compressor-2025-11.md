# Napa Resort Compressor Service Report

## Reported condition

The refrigeration controller reported intermittent low suction pressure during morning startup. Site staff observed no unusual sound and no visible refrigerant plume.

## Findings

The technician found oil staining at the suction service-valve cap. Electronic leak detection confirmed a small leak at the cap seal. Controller pressure differed from the calibrated manifold by 2 percent, within the service tolerance. Condenser airflow and evaporator condition were normal.

## Corrective action

The cap seal was replaced, the connection was leak-tested, and the circuit charge was verified without adding refrigerant. The technician monitored three start cycles with stable suction pressure. Reinspect the service-valve cap at the next preventive-maintenance visit.
