# Standby Generator Start-Failure Guide

## Safe initial inspection

Place the transfer switch in the approved test state and follow site lockout procedures. Check the controller event log before clearing alarms. Inspect battery terminals, measure resting battery voltage, verify charger output, and confirm the emergency-stop circuit is closed.

## Crank but no start

Verify fuel level and shutoff position, inspect for water in the separator, and confirm the engine speed signal during crank. Do not bypass a fuel solenoid or safety interlock. Escalate repeated overspeed, low oil pressure, or controller communication faults.

## Functional test

After repair, run the generator unloaded and then under the site-approved test load. Record crank time, running voltage, frequency, oil pressure, coolant temperature, and transfer-switch behavior.
