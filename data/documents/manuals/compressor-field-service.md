# Compressor Field Service Manual

## Safety and isolation

Only an EPA refrigerant-certified technician may open the refrigerant circuit. Confirm electrical isolation, apply lockout/tagout, and verify zero energy before removing guards or opening control panels. Do not reset a pressure safety more than once without identifying the cause.

## Initial diagnostic sequence

For a reported pressure loss, first compare suction and discharge pressure with the controller trend and a calibrated manifold. Inspect accessible fittings, service valves, and oil traces for signs of leakage. Confirm that condenser airflow is unobstructed and that the evaporator is not iced before adding refrigerant or changing a setpoint.

If measured pressure disagrees with the controller by more than 5 percent, test the pressure transducer and wiring before condemning the compressor. Record ambient temperature, line temperatures, measured pressures, and alarm history.

## Escalation conditions

Stop the compressor and escalate when there is a rapid pressure drop, evidence of a major refrigerant release, repeated low-pressure trips after one controlled reset, abnormal mechanical noise, or motor winding overheating. Preserve controller logs and photographs with the service record.
