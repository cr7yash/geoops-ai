# Rooftop Unit Airflow Troubleshooting Guide

## First checks

Before replacing controls, confirm the supply fan command, belt condition, filter differential pressure, damper position, and visible coil blockage. Compare the variable-frequency drive output with measured motor speed. A dirty filter or closed outside-air damper can create an airflow alarm while the fan remains operational.

## Sensor validation

Inspect the airflow sensor tubing for kinks, moisture, and loose connections. With the fan stopped, the differential pressure reading should return to zero. Recalibrate only after mechanical restrictions and tubing faults are ruled out.

## Return to service

After correction, operate the unit through occupied and unoccupied modes. Record supply airflow, static pressure, fan speed, filter pressure drop, and damper command in the service report.
