# Redwood Health Network Field Service Agreement

## Priority response commitments

Critical incidents require acknowledgement within two hours and an on-site response within four hours. High-priority incidents require acknowledgement within four hours and an on-site response within eight hours. These targets use the incident creation timestamp and the customer's site timezone.

## Qualified personnel

Work involving refrigerant circuits must be assigned to a technician whose EPA refrigerant certification remains valid through the scheduled service window. Generator work requires the Generator Systems II certification. Substitution with an uncertified technician is not permitted to avoid an SLA breach.

## Escalation

If the on-site target is likely to be missed, the operations lead must notify the Redwood facilities contact before the deadline and record the reason, recovery plan, and next update time. Travel delay does not suspend the response clock.
