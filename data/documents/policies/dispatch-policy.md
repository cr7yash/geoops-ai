# Field Dispatch Eligibility Policy

## Mandatory gates

A dispatch candidate must be available, hold every required certification through the proposed service window, have no overlapping active or scheduled assignment, and have valid location evidence. Candidates without a usable route or with a projected travel time above the configured limit are excluded.

## Ranking evidence

Eligible candidates are ranked using certification match, route travel time, workload, historical performance, and completed-job experience. An operator must be able to inspect the score components and every exclusion reason. Ranking may not override a mandatory gate.

## Mutations

Recommendations are read-only. Creating, changing, or cancelling an assignment requires a separate approval workflow and must not occur from a retrieval or recommendation endpoint.
