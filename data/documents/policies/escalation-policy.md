# Service Escalation and Communication Policy

## SLA risk

Escalate a ticket when the remaining SLA window is shorter than projected travel plus the initial diagnostic allowance, when no eligible technician is available, or when a route provider cannot supply reliable travel evidence. Record the constraint instead of assigning an ineligible technician.

## Safety escalation

Safety interlocks, refrigerant releases, electrical arcing, fire-system impairment, and uncontrolled equipment shutdowns require immediate supervisor notification. Preserve the scene and do not bypass protective devices to restore service.

## Customer updates

Customer updates must identify the current condition, operational impact, dispatched resource or blocking constraint, next action, and next update time. Do not state a root cause until supported by field evidence.
